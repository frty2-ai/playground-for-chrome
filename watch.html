Frty2 Watch

Recordings of an expert using an application while explaining it. Each one is saved on this computer as it is made, and uploads to Frty2 while you record when Watch is linked to an expert session (or you are signed in as a Backstage author). Once it is uploaded, Frty2 turns it into lessons and a link for learners by itself: this page shows how far it has got.

Let the tutor see your screen

When you ask the tutor something, it looks at your screen once to answer you. Chrome needs your permission for that. Nothing is seen when you are not talking to it.
